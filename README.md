# X Premium vs Premium Plus: Which Tier Fits Your Posting, Browsing, and Grok Needs?

Choosing between **X Premium** and **Premium Plus** looks simple until you compare the price gap with the features you will actually use. Premium starts at **$8 per month or $84 per year** on the web, while Premium+ starts at **$40 per month or $395 per year** in the United States. That makes Premium+ $32 more per month, or $311 more per year when billed annually.

For most creators, professionals, and ordinary X users, Premium covers the features that matter most: the blue checkmark after eligibility review, longer posts and videos, fewer ads in selected timelines, reply prioritization, Articles, Media Studio, and access to monetization applications. Premium+ is aimed at heavier users who specifically need a largely ad-free X experience, the highest reply prioritization, X Pro, and expanded Grok-related access under X's current official plan description.

There is one important detail if you are considering a third-party subscription service such as WildAI: its current X help documentation says that the X service is monthly-only and separately states that Grok is not included in its X Premium+ product. That differs from X's own current feature page, which lists SuperGrok and Grok Bot as Premium+ benefits. Check the exact product description and checkout details before paying.

## Quick Verdict

Choose **X Premium** if you want:

- A blue checkmark, subject to X's eligibility review
- Longer posts and video uploads
- Articles and Media Studio
- Approximately 50% fewer ads in the For You and Following timelines
- Access to apply for Original Content Rewards and Creator Subscriptions
- A lower-cost subscription for regular posting

Choose **Premium+** if you will consistently use:

- A mostly ad-free X browsing experience
- The highest reply prioritization level
- X Pro for multiple timelines and advanced search
- Expanded Grok access, if it is included in the purchase channel and region
- X as a primary work, research, publishing, or customer-acquisition tool

If you only want a blue checkmark or the ability to edit posts, Premium+ is difficult to justify. The extra $32 per month pays for a narrower set of high-end benefits, not a completely different social network.

## What You Get With X Premium

X Premium is the middle tier, but it contains most of the practical upgrades that active users are looking for.

### Longer Posts and Video Uploads

Premium allows subscribers to create posts of up to **25,000 characters**. It also supports longer video uploads, with X currently describing uploads of up to approximately three hours and 8 GB at 1080p on supported web and iOS experiences. Availability can depend on platform and location.

That matters for writers, analysts, journalists, educators, and businesses that regularly run out of the standard character limit. If your normal posts are short, this feature may sit untouched. If you publish threads that keep turning into awkward chains, it can be useful immediately.

### Edit Posts

Premium includes a limited editing window for published original posts and quote posts. X says the editing window is one hour, with a limited number of changes available.

This is helpful for correcting links, fixing typos, or updating a sentence after publication. It does not turn a post into a fully editable document, and it does not remove the need to check important claims before posting.

### Fewer Ads, But Not an Ad-Free Timeline

Premium reduces ads by approximately 50% in the For You and Following timelines. X does not promise that all promoted content disappears, and the reduced-ads benefit does not cover every part of the platform. Ads may still appear in areas such as profiles, post replies, Explore promotions, and other placements.

This distinction is easy to miss. Premium makes browsing less interrupted in the main timelines, but it is not the right tier if your main goal is to remove advertising across most of X.

### Blue Checkmark Eligibility

Premium includes access to the blue checkmark for accounts that meet X's review and eligibility requirements. The checkmark is not necessarily displayed immediately after payment, and X can remove it if an account fails its requirements or violates platform rules.

The checkmark can matter for public-facing accounts, but it should not be treated as an automatic credibility certificate. It indicates a paid subscription combined with X's eligibility process.

### Creator and Publishing Tools

Premium includes access to features such as:

- Articles for long-form publishing
- Media Studio
- Larger reply prioritization
- ID verification
- Applications for Original Content Rewards
- Applications for Creator Subscriptions
- Premium gifting

X says monetization features remain subject to separate eligibility requirements. Paying for Premium does not guarantee approval or a payout.

For people who publish regularly, these tools are often more relevant than the badge itself. A creator who uses Articles, longer uploads, and monetization tools may get reasonable value from Premium without moving to Premium+.

## What Premium+ Adds

Premium+ includes Premium's core benefits and adds several features designed for high-frequency users.

### A More Extensive Ad-Free Experience

X describes Premium+ as offering an ad-free experience across most areas of the platform, including the For You timeline, Following timeline, post replies, and profiles. X also notes that sponsored content may still appear occasionally.

That is a meaningful upgrade if you use X for research, monitoring, customer support, or long browsing sessions. It is less meaningful if you open the platform briefly, publish a post, and leave.

The price difference is large enough that you should calculate this as a workflow decision. Paying $40 per month to avoid a few ads during occasional browsing is probably unnecessary. Paying it to keep a monitoring dashboard clean for several hours a day is a different calculation.

### Highest Reply Prioritization

Both Premium and Premium+ offer reply prioritization, but Premium+ receives the strongest level. This can matter to accounts that rely on replies for visibility, customer conversations, or audience growth.

It is not a guarantee that every reply will perform well. Reply ranking is only useful when you are actively participating in conversations, and it cannot replace relevant posts, timely responses, or an audience that cares about the topic.

### X Pro

X's current Premium+ description includes access to X Pro, which allows users to view multiple configurable timelines at once, use advanced search, and manage certain contributor or admin functions.

X Pro is the clearest reason for some professionals to choose Premium+. It is designed for people who follow several accounts, keywords, lists, or topics at the same time. For example, a social media manager might monitor a brand account, competitor mentions, customer replies, breaking news, and a saved search in separate columns.

If you only use one timeline and search occasionally, X Pro will probably feel like an expensive dashboard you rarely open.

### Grok and SuperGrok

X's official feature page currently lists higher SuperGrok access and Grok Bot for Premium+. It also mentions features such as image and video generation and voice mode, subject to platform and location availability.

However, WildAI's own X subscription help article says that Grok and X Premium/Premium+ are separate products in its ordering system. It specifically advises customers that subscribing to X Premium+ through WildAI does not include Grok and that Grok must be ordered separately.

That conflict is important. The benefit attached to an official X Premium+ subscription may not be identical to what a reseller fulfills under its own product listing. Before buying through WildAI, confirm:

1. Whether the product is an official X Premium+ subscription or a separate fulfillment arrangement.
2. Whether SuperGrok is included in the displayed price.
3. Whether Grok access is available for your account region.
4. Whether the checkout description matches the features you expect.
5. Whether the subscription is monthly-only.

## Premium vs Premium Plus for Different Types of Users

### Casual X Users

For casual users, neither paid tier is automatically necessary. If you mainly read posts, follow friends, and occasionally publish a short update, the free version may be enough.

If you want to edit posts or reduce ads in the main timelines, Premium is the more sensible upgrade. Premium+ adds features that casual users may not open often enough to justify the price.

### Individual Creators

Premium is usually the better starting point for creators. It includes longer posts, long-form publishing through Articles, fewer ads, a blue checkmark subject to review, and access to monetization applications.

Premium+ becomes more interesting when X is a major distribution channel and you regularly use high reply prioritization, X Pro, or advanced Grok access. A creator who posts once or twice per week probably does not need the top tier simply because it has a longer feature list.

### Social Media Managers

Premium+ has a stronger case for social media professionals because X Pro's multi-column workflow can reduce the need to switch between separate searches and lists. The value depends on how much time you spend monitoring multiple streams.

Before choosing Premium+, compare its price with the cost of your existing social media tools. If you already pay for a scheduler, social inbox, or monitoring platform, X Pro may not replace enough of that workflow to justify the additional subscription cost.

### Researchers and News Monitors

Premium+ is more suitable for users who spend long periods inside X and need a cleaner, more configurable monitoring environment. The mostly ad-free experience and multi-column layout are the main attractions.

Premium still works well for researchers who mainly need longer posts, Articles, fewer ads in the primary timelines, and the ability to search manually.

### Grok Users

If Grok is the main reason you are considering Premium+, verify the exact entitlement through the purchase channel. X's official documentation associates SuperGrok with Premium+, while WildAI's help documentation says its X Premium+ order does not include Grok.

Do not rely on the plan name alone. Read the checkout description and confirm whether Grok is included before payment.

## Is Premium+ Worth the Extra Cost?

The direct price comparison is:

- Premium: $8 per month
- Premium+: $40 per month
- Monthly difference: $32
- Premium annual price: $84
- Premium+ annual price: $395
- Annual difference: $311

The extra cost is not buying a slightly better version of the same plan. It is mainly paying for:

- More aggressive ad removal
- Stronger reply prioritization
- X Pro
- Expanded Grok-related access on eligible official subscriptions

Premium+ may be worth it when at least one of these benefits is central to your work. It is harder to justify when you only want the checkmark, longer posts, or the ability to edit a typo.

A practical test is to write down the three features you will use every week. If the list contains only editing, longer posts, and fewer ads in the main feed, Premium is probably enough. If it includes X Pro every day, extensive monitoring, and a need for the highest visibility level, Premium+ has a clearer case.

## Common Questions

### Is X Premium the same as Premium Plus?

No. X Premium is the middle consumer tier, while Premium+ is the highest standard consumer tier. Premium+ includes Premium's core features and adds stronger ad removal, higher reply prioritization, X Pro, and additional benefits described by X's current plan documentation.

### Does Premium include a blue checkmark?

Premium includes eligibility for the blue checkmark, but X requires account review and other conditions. The badge is not guaranteed simply because the subscription payment succeeds.

### Does Premium remove all ads?

No. Premium reduces ads by approximately 50% in the For You and Following timelines. Ads can remain elsewhere on X. Premium+ offers a much broader ad-free experience, although X still notes that sponsored content may occasionally appear.

### Does Premium+ include Grok?

For a direct official X Premium+ subscription, X currently lists SuperGrok and Grok Bot as Premium+ benefits. WildAI's own help documentation says its X Premium+ product does not include Grok and that Grok is separate. Verify the exact purchase channel and checkout terms before ordering.

### Can I pay annually through WildAI?

WildAI's current X help article says its service supports monthly subscriptions only. X itself lists both monthly and annual web pricing, but the availability of annual billing through a third-party fulfillment service is a separate question.

### Which plan is better for most people?

Premium is the better fit for most users who want paid X features. Premium+ is mainly for people who will actively use X Pro, need a mostly ad-free working environment, or have a verified need for its additional Grok-related features.

## Final Recommendation

Pick **X Premium** if your priority is publishing more comfortably, editing posts, reducing ads in the main timelines, accessing Articles and Media Studio, or applying for creator monetization programs.

Pick **Premium+** only when its extra tools match your routine. X Pro, stronger reply prioritization, and a mostly ad-free interface can be valuable for professionals who work inside X every day. They are expensive additions for someone who only wants a badge or longer posts.

When ordering through WildAI, treat the displayed checkout details as decisive. Confirm the exact plan, billing period, included features, and final price before completing payment.
